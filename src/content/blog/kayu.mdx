# Coffee
```text
Roaster: Sweet Bloom Coffee Roasters, Denver CO
Coffee: Kayu 
Country: Kenya
Origin: Murang'a
Process: Washed
Elevation: 1650 MSAL
Varietal: Ruiri
Producer: Various small plot growers
Flavor profile: Blood orange, assam, butterscotch
```

# Brew 2: The Virtuous Pour
```text
- Method: pour over
- Beans: 29g
- Water: 500mL
- Grind: Medium-fine
```

## Black
- clean
- subtle, but full flavors
- assam comes through

## Cream
- rich toffee/butterscotch flavor goes well with the cream
- very smooth

# Brew 3: A Fair Cap
```text
- Method: cappuccino
- Beans: 18g
- Milk: 134g
```
- delicious toffee flavor
- smooth & creamy
- delightful
- very subtle tartness
